App: ShelfTap catalogue, for library members to browse and search the books of a library. Visual direction: modern and polished, a gradient hero header, a grid of book covers, a light and a dark theme that follow the system setting, rounded semi-transparent cards (a static translucent fill, no blur), restrained motion.

Data (there is no server): a local read-only catalogue of 5,000 generated titles, produced by a deterministic generator in code (fixed seed) at first use, never on the main thread. A title has id (string), isbn (string), title (string), authors (list of strings), year (integer), subjects (list of strings), copiesAvailable (integer). A cover is drawn locally from the title id (a gradient tile with the title initials); no image is downloaded.

Screens:
1. Catalogue: a hero header, then a grid of covers with title and author under each, and an "Available" or "All copies out" badge (copiesAvailable > 0). It scrolls through all titles.
2. Search: a search field above the grid. The grid updates as the member types, matching title, author, ISBN or subject; the filtering runs off the main thread and is debounced. An empty result shows "No books found".
3. Title detail: opened from a cover. Shows the large cover, all fields, the subjects as chips, and a back button.
Navigation: a bottom bar with Catalogue and Search, and the detail screen on top.
